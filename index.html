About the shop

Tim Vanwey started JOLT in 2005 as JOLT CAD & Consulting, doing machine design, reverse engineering, CAD/CAM, and consulting work.

In 2010 it became JOLT LLC, a full fabrication and machine shop. Our crew has over 50 years of combined experience in machining and fabrication, mostly for coal mining, automotive, and agriculture customers.
