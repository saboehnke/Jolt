Who We Are

Built from
the ground
up

JOLT was established in 2005 as JOLT CAD & Consulting by Tim Vanwey — delivering machine design, reverse engineering, CAD/CAM, and consulting services.

In 2010 we expanded into JOLT LLC, a full-service fabrication and machine shop. We've continued to grow in both technology and capability ever since.

Our team brings over 50 years of combined experience in machining and fabrication, serving coal mining, automotive, and agriculture industries.
